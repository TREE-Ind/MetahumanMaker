# Community conduct

We want artists, engineers and agent builders with different backgrounds to be able to contribute productively.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique the work without attacking the person.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information or spam project spaces.
- Respect consent, asset rights and privacy when sharing character references and examples.

Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive contributions or restrict participation to protect the community. Raise sensitive concerns through an available private maintainer channel; do not publish private details in an issue. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
